# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Description
The Stakeholder Liaison bridges communication between the delivery team and external stakeholders to ensure expectations, updates, and concerns are shared clearly and on time.

### Key Responsibilities
- Maintain a stakeholder map and communication cadence
- Translate project status into stakeholder-friendly updates
- Gather stakeholder concerns and route them to the right owners
- Coordinate messaging with the Communications Lead
- Support Project Manager with stakeholder alignment before key milestones

### Decision-Making Authority
- Select communication channels and meeting cadence for stakeholder groups
- Prioritize stakeholder follow-ups based on project impact
- Escalate communication risks to the Project Manager when alignment is at risk

### Interaction Matrix
| Role | Interaction |
| --- | --- |
| Project Manager | Reports status themes, alignment risks, and escalation needs |
| Product Managers | Aligns customer-facing messaging with roadmap priorities |
| Developers | Collects technical clarifications required for stakeholder updates |
| Communications Lead | Coordinates timing, tone, and distribution of communications |
| External Stakeholders | Provides updates, captures feedback, and confirms expectations |

### Required Skills and Competencies
- Stakeholder management and relationship building
- Clear written and verbal communication
- Conflict resolution and active listening
- Meeting facilitation and follow-through discipline

---

## Risk Owner

### Role Description
The Risk Owner is accountable for identifying, assessing, and monitoring project risks, ensuring mitigations are planned and escalations occur before risks become delivery failures.

### Key Responsibilities
- Identify risks across scope, timeline, resources, and dependencies
- Maintain and update the risk register with likelihood and impact
- Define mitigation and contingency actions with accountable owners
- Monitor risk triggers and report status regularly
- Escalate high-severity risks to Project Manager and Executive Sponsor

### Decision-Making Authority
- Classify risk severity and response priority
- Trigger risk escalations based on agreed thresholds
- Recommend mitigation strategies and contingency activation

### Interaction Matrix
| Role | Interaction |
| --- | --- |
| Project Manager | Aligns risk response plans with project plans and status reporting |
| Executive Sponsor | Escalates strategic/high-impact risks and decision needs |
| Product Managers | Assesses risks related to scope and value trade-offs |
| Developers | Reviews technical risks, dependencies, and mitigation feasibility |

### Required Skills and Competencies
- Risk analysis and scenario planning
- Data-driven decision support
- Escalation judgment and prioritization
- Strong documentation and tracking habits

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Description
The Quality Assurance Lead defines and enforces quality standards across delivery activities so releases meet functional, performance, and reliability expectations.

### Key Responsibilities
- Define project quality criteria and acceptance gates
- Oversee test strategy across functional and non-functional areas
- Coordinate defect triage and closure priorities
- Monitor quality metrics and release readiness
- Ensure lessons learned are fed back into quality practices

### Decision-Making Authority
- Approve or block release readiness based on quality criteria
- Set testing scope and quality gate thresholds
- Prioritize critical defect remediation in coordination with delivery leads

### Interaction Matrix
| Role | Interaction |
| --- | --- |
| Development Lead | Aligns testability, quality gates, and defect resolution priorities |
| Project Manager | Reports quality status, risks, and release readiness decisions |
| Developers | Provides quality standards, test expectations, and defect feedback |
| Product Managers | Confirms acceptance criteria quality and customer-impact trade-offs |

### Required Skills and Competencies
- Test strategy and quality engineering fundamentals
- Root-cause analysis and defect management
- Metrics interpretation and release governance
- Cross-functional collaboration and coaching

---

## Change Management Coordinator

### Role Description
The Change Management Coordinator manages scope and requirement changes, ensuring impacts are evaluated, documented, and approved before implementation.

### Key Responsibilities
- Operate the change intake and tracking process
- Perform impact analysis for timeline, cost, risk, and quality
- Facilitate change review discussions and approval workflows
- Maintain change logs and decision history
- Communicate approved changes and downstream actions

### Decision-Making Authority
- Determine change request completeness and readiness for review
- Recommend approve/defer/reject outcomes based on impact analysis
- Enforce adherence to agreed change control process

### Interaction Matrix
| Role | Interaction |
| --- | --- |
| Project Manager | Aligns approved changes with project plan and delivery commitments |
| Development Lead | Validates technical effort, sequencing, and implementation risk |
| Stakeholder Liaison | Confirms stakeholder expectations and communication needs for change outcomes |
| Product Managers | Assesses product value impact and prioritization implications |

### Required Skills and Competencies
- Change control and impact analysis
- Process governance and documentation rigor
- Facilitation and negotiation
- Dependency and schedule awareness

---

## Knowledge Transfer Specialist

### Role Description
The Knowledge Transfer Specialist ensures institutional knowledge is captured and shared through documentation, training, and handover activities across the project lifecycle.

### Key Responsibilities
- Define and maintain project knowledge capture standards
- Coordinate documentation updates for key decisions and processes
- Create or facilitate training and handover sessions
- Validate completeness of operational runbooks and support guides
- Track knowledge transfer readiness before major milestones

### Decision-Making Authority
- Define required documentation and handover artifacts
- Gate milestone completion on agreed knowledge transfer criteria
- Recommend remediation actions when knowledge gaps are identified

### Interaction Matrix
| Role | Interaction |
| --- | --- |
| Project Manager | Reports knowledge transfer status and readiness risks |
| Developers | Captures technical implementation details and operational guidance |
| Product Managers | Documents business context, decisions, and success measures |
| Quality Assurance Lead | Aligns quality evidence and lessons learned into reusable artifacts |
| Support/Operations Teams | Coordinates handover readiness and adoption feedback |

### Required Skills and Competencies
- Technical and process documentation excellence
- Training design and facilitation
- Information organization and lifecycle management
- Cross-team communication and knowledge stewardship
